Our Story

A landmark of Gawal Mandi

Standing at Gawal Mandi Chowk on Chamberlain Road, in the heart of Lahore's historic Anarkali Bazaar, Punjab Bakery is one of those old-city names that generations of Lahoris simply know. In a bazaar that has fed the city for as long as anyone can remember, the bakery's ovens are part of the rhythm of the neighbourhood.

Everything here is baked fresh in-house. Two things, above all, carry the bakery's name across Lahore: its Butter Buns and its Almond Khataaies — the simple, honest baking that people cross the old city for.

No shortcuts, no fuss — just a traditional bakery doing what it has always done: baking fresh in-house for the people of Lahore.
